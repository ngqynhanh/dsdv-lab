Density legend

    Fixed design thresholds: 100, 500 and 1,000 people/km². Exact boundary values enter the higher band.

    Highlighted circles have a dark outline. Other circles remain visible but faded. Selecting “All provinces” restores the overview.

    Limitation

    Overlapping circles can still conceal provinces, especially where many selected provinces cluster. Fading other categories helps but does not separate overlapping marks.

    Source: supplied province CSV. Population: thousand people; GRDP: million VND/person/year; area: km²; density: people/km². Source values are preserved.
