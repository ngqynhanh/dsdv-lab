CHANGE BLINDNESS
Detail of an image cannot be remembered across separate scenes
except in areas with focused attention
Interruption (e.g. a blink, eye saccade or blank screen) amplifies 
this effect
Not failure of vision system
Failure due to inappropriate attentional guidance

Overwriting: information that was not abstracted is lost

•
Feature combination: scenes are combined as long as they make
