=== PAGE 60 ===
EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS
1. No difference for interpretation accuracy
2. No difference in recall accuracy after a five-
minute gap
3. Significantly better recall for Holmes charts of 
both the chart topic and the details (categories and 
trend) after long-term gap (2-3 weeks).
4. Participants saw value messages in the 
Holmes charts significantly more often than in the 
plain charts.
5. Participants found the Holmes charts more 
attractive, most enjoyed them, and found that 
they were easiest and fastest to remember.
