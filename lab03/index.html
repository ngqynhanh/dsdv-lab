    Reading the chart

    Both axes start at zero because population and income have meaningful zero baselines. Upper limits come from the loaded data.

    Radius is proportional to the square root of land area, so the visible circle area is proportional to province area.

    Transparent fills and thin outlines help reveal overlap. Every valid record is retained; some circles can still obscure others. Hover over a circle to see its province name, population, GRDP, area and density.

    Source: vn-provinces-data.csv, supplied by the lab. Population: thousand people; GRDP: million VND/person/year; land area: km²; density: people/km². Values are displayed as supplied, without correcting possible source anomalies.

    14. Homework — Tooltips and interpretation

    Hover over a circle to see its province name, population, GRDP per person, land area and population density.
