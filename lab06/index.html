Extension — daily new confirmed cases (stacked)

Each band is one country's daily new cases (today's cumulative count minus yesterday's). The top edge is the four-country total for that day. Negative revisions are shown as 0.

Source: JHU CSSE COVID-19 Data Repository — time_series_covid19_confirmed_global.csv. Historical data (1 Apr – 1 May 2020); country totals are the sum of all province/region rows.
